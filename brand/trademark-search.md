# Trademark search sheet: glint

This is not a clearance. It lists where to look before you use the name and mark in commerce. Get a clearance search
from a trademark lawyer before you file or spend money on the brand.

## The name

- USPTO trademark search: https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/ (search "glint" and close spellings)
- WIPO Global Brand Database: https://branddb.wipo.int/ (all countries you sell in)
- EUIPO eSearch: https://euipo.europa.eu/eSearch/
- Classes to search: 9, 42

## The mark

Search the design codes (the Vienna Classification in WIPO's database, the USPTO's design search codes) for what the
mark shows: a solid, irregular rounded shape (a pebble or drop) with a four-pointed star cut out of it, high on the
left. Look under stars with four points, and under other figures with a star inside them.
Look for marks in the same classes that would read as the same at a glance.

The mark was not traced from another logo. Its outline comes from the pebble shape Glint draws on its Home page
(`crates/glint/src/ui/outline.rs`, the same maths as the shader), and the star is the slim four-pointed glint.
