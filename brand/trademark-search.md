# Trademark search sheet: glint

This is a starting checklist, not legal clearance. Ask a trademark lawyer for a full clearance search before registering or using the name and mark commercially.

## Name searches

Search "glint" and close spellings in classes 9 (software) and 42 (software services):

- USPTO: https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/
- WIPO Global Brand Database: https://branddb.wipo.int/
- EUIPO eSearch: https://euipo.europa.eu/eSearch/

## Mark searches

Search the WIPO Vienna Classification and USPTO design codes for an irregular rounded pebble shape with a four-pointed star cut out of the upper-left side (categories for four-pointed stars and geometric/organic shapes containing stars) in classes 9 and 42.

The mark is original to this project: its outer contour comes from the pebble outline in `crates/glint/src/ui/outline.rs`, paired with a four-pointed star cut-out.
